## AncientGuardianRegenSystem

```csharp
[UpdateInGroup(typeof(FFFixedPostTransformGroup))]
public struct AncientGuardianRegenSystem : ISystem, ISystemCompilerGenerated
```

The Ancient Guardian heals a fraction of its max health per second (its config's HealingRateFP) continuously, in and out of combat, so it cannot be whittled down across repeated deaths and retreats. Unlike `FFSystems.Combat.HealthRegenSystem` there is no post-damage delay, and the Guardian carries no HealthRegenData, so the two never both heal it.
