| `public static fp3 RotateTowards(fp3 forward, fp3 goalDirection, fp maxTurnRadians)` | Rotates `forward` toward `goalDirection` by at most `maxTurnRadians`, the fixed-point equivalent of the float mover's `Quaternion.RotateTowards` on the heading.
